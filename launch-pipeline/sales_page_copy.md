## Full sales page copy

### You don't need more willpower. You need a plan that doesn't fight you.

If you've ever white-knuckled your way through a diet — cut out every
"bad" food, felt strong for a few days, then caved into a binge that
felt bigger than the one before — that's not a discipline problem.
It's what happens to almost everyone on a diet with a food on a banned
list. The second something's forbidden, you want it more. Psychologists
call it reactance. You've been fighting your own brain, and your brain
was always going to win.

**The Anything Meal Method doesn't ban anything.** It gives your
cravings a scheduled place in the week instead — so there's nothing to
white-knuckle, and nothing to binge on.

### How it works

Four days of simple, structured meals. Three days that include one
**Anything Meal** — a real dinner, whatever you want, guilt-free,
already on the calendar.

- **Mon–Thu (Core Routine):** breakfast, lunch, and dinner are
  nourishing, plant-forward meals — mostly "peel, cut, and eat." No
  elaborate cooking, no recipes to master.
- **Fri–Sun:** breakfast and lunch stay on the core routine. Dinner is
  your Anything Meal.

Not unlimited freedom — *scheduled* freedom. A meal you know is coming
is satisfying. Unlimited freedom is where binges live. And because it's
scheduled instead of earned, there's no "good" week to ruin and nothing
to feel guilty about when you sit down to it.

If four structured days sounds like a lot right now: start with two,
and add a day each week. A plan you actually follow beats a "perfect"
one you quit by Thursday.

## Optional FAQ block (add if Payhip page feels short, or as a PDF appendix)

**Do I need to count calories or macros?**
No. The structure does the work — you're not tracking numbers.

**What if I miss a Core Routine day?**
Pick it back up the next day. One off day doesn't undo the method —
that's the whole point of scheduling flexibility instead of banning
foods.

**Is this vegan / keto / gluten-free?**
The Core Routine is plant-forward with a protein-or-carb dinner
rotation — it's adaptable to most preferences, but it's not written as
a strict vegan or keto plan.

**Can I do this if I don't cook much?**
Yes — Core Routine meals are intentionally simple (juice/smoothie,
fruit, one-pan dinner). No elaborate recipes required.
