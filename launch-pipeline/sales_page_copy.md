# Payhip listing — copy/paste package

Everything below the line is written to paste straight into Payhip's
fields with no cleanup. No markdown symbols in the paste blocks (`**`,
`###`) — Payhip's description editor is a plain rich-text box, so
those would just paste as literal asterisks. Bullets use `•` so they
look right whether or not you bold anything. If you want section
headers bold, select each header line after pasting and hit Bold in
the toolbar — optional, reads fine either way.

**SEO approach:** Payhip listings get indexed by Google and by
Payhip's own on-site search, so the product name, summary, and first
150ish words of the description carry the most weight. I pulled the
target phrases from how this exact buyer actually searches — "stop
binge eating," "sustainable weight loss," "meal plan without cheat
days," "yo-yo dieting" — plus your guide's own cover line ("weight
loss without giving up the meals you love"), which is a strong,
literal long-tail match. I left "intuitive eating" and "anti-diet" out
of the title/description/tags on purpose — same reasoning as the
launch content: the guide has real food rules and a weight-loss goal,
so ranking for and being clicked on by that exact search term is more
likely to produce refunds and bad reviews than sales, from an audience
that will open the PDF and immediately spot the mismatch.

Not unlimited freedom — scheduled freedom. A meal you know is coming is satisfying. Unlimited freedom is where binges live. And because it's scheduled instead of earned, there's no "good" week to ruin and nothing to feel guilty about when you sit down to it.
